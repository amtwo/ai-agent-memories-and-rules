## Decision record guidance

- Ask the owner before retiring a feature, changing a public URL, excluding content, importing historical analytics, or scheduling public content.
- Record the owner's choice, relevant limits, and a concrete follow-up action.
- Put private backup paths and account details only in access-controlled project notes.
- Revisit a decision when new evidence changes its assumptions.
